# SSMU-Data-Challenge-I

Welcome to Data Challenge I! You and your teammates will have from 11 am to 5:30 pm on Saturday, October 17th, to explore and submit a presentation about your data analysis.

This Git repository contains this `README` file and the dataset in a `CSV` format. If you are a Duke student, we recommend you use Duke's Container Manager to access your preferred IDE.

## How to Participate

1. Fork the repository emailed to you by susan.li@duke.edu
2. Load the data from `motor_vehicle_collisions.zip`. Click on the file, click “View Raw”, and download.
3. Come up with a question to answer with data analysis. Analyze the dataset and prepare a presentation of your findings.
4. Submit your files to your team’s Google Drive folder. Submission materials should include: your code notebook file, presentation media, and citations to all outside resources.
5. Present! **You must fit your presentation within a 5-minute timeframe**. Please do not attempt to push changes to this repository - it is read-only for participants.
The analysis period will run from ~11:30 am to 4:30 pm. You and your team may stay in the room, Physics 150, or leave during the analysis period (to get food, analyze in another room, etc.). You and your team must be back in Physics 150 by 4:30 pm sharp to submit your presentation materials.

## Data Dictionary

**About**

You will be working with is NYC motor vehicle collision data. The Motor Vehicle Collisions data tables contain information from all police-reported motor vehicle collisions in NYC. Each row represents a crash event. The police report (MV104-AN) is required to be filled out for collisions where someone is injured or killed, or where there is at least $1000 worth of damage. The dataset includes X records across 29 columns and uniquely identifies observations by the X column.

Data Source: City of New York

Last Updated: July 31, 2026

Link: https://catalog.data.gov/dataset/motor-vehicle-collisions-crashes
